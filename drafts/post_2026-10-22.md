Consolidating thousands of daily logistics events into ERP for financial posting isn't trivial. High-volume WMS/OMS often generate granular transaction data (shipments, adjustments, freight charges) that, if directly posted, overload ERPs like NetSuite or SAP. The result: performance bottlenecks, reconciliation nightmares, and delayed financial closes. This isn't just a data mapping problem; it's a 𝘁𝗿𝗮𝗻𝘀𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗮𝗴𝗴𝗿𝗲𝗴𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗴𝘆 challenge.

Implement an intermediate 𝗳𝗶𝗻𝗮𝗻𝗰𝗶𝗮𝗹 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗹𝗮𝘆𝗲𝗿 or aggregation service. This layer should:
*   Collect granular logistics events.
*   Apply business rules for aggregation (e.g., consolidate all shipments from a single day into one journal entry per GL account).
*   Generate summarized postings (e.g., aggregated revenue, COGS, freight expense) consumable by ERP APIs or flat files.
*   Ensure traceability back to original logistics transactions.

How are you managing the reconciliation challenge when high-volume logistics hits your ERP's GL?

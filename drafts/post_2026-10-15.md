Integrating Robotics & Automation Control Systems (RCS) directly with WMS requires precise low-latency communication, not just data mapping.

Traditional asynchronous message queues, while robust for business events, can introduce unacceptable delays when orchestrating physical movements or reacting to real-time sensor data from autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) or automated storage and retrieval systems (AS/RS). A late command or stale WMS inventory view can halt operations.

*   𝗘𝘃𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗦𝘁𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗺𝘀: Leverage Kafka or similar platforms for telemetry, sensor data, and state changes *from* the RCS to WMS, decoupling high-volume data ingest from critical command paths.
*   𝗢𝗽𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗰 𝗖𝗼𝗻𝗰𝘂𝗿𝗿𝗲𝗻𝗰𝘆: Implement within the WMS for inventory updates driven by robot movements, resolving conflicts at scale.

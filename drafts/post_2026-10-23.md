A common pitfall is treating each system update as an isolated event. This leads to data inconsistencies when downstream failures occur, leaving orders in limbo or inventory out of sync. True end-to-end fulfillment often spans multiple integration points, requiring a robust strategy beyond simple retries. We're talking about guaranteed consistency for complex processes like an entire order-to-shipment lifecycle.

The challenge isn't just moving data; it's maintaining a single, immutable state for returns. *   WMS consumes, processes physical return.
*   WMS publishes `ITEM_RECEIVED_AND_DISPOSED` event with condition codes.
*   ERP consumes, generates credit memo.

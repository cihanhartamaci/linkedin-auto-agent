Navigating dynamic order changes and cancellations within traditional EDI 850-940-945 workflows is a significant integration bottleneck.

When an ERP issues an 850 Purchase Order, and a WMS processes it via 940/945, real-time changes are problematic. A customer might cancel a line item (EDI 860) or change quantity after the 940 is sent but before the 945 confirms shipment.

Traditional middleware often struggles with elastic demand spikes and maintaining state across disconnected systems. An event-driven approach, powered by serverless functions (e.g., AWS Lambda, Azure Functions), decouples WMS, OMS, and ERP interactions. This allows individual integration workflows to scale independently based on actual load, rather than provisioning for peak capacity. It’s critical for peak seasons or sudden volume increases.

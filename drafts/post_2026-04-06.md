Managing data egress costs in hybrid cloud logistics isn't a minor optimization; it's a critical architectural decision affecting Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) and real-time analytics viability. When moving multi-terabyte daily WMS transaction logs or historical OMS data for analytics and reporting from cloud to on-prem (or inter-cloud), default egress pricing models can devastate budgets. Beyond cost, network latency often bottlenecks crucial data pipelines for demand forecasting or supply chain visibility.

Architectural strategy must deliberately target these challenges. Consider implementing:
*   𝗘𝗱𝗴𝗲 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗰𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴: Filter and aggregate data closer to the source before egress.
*   𝗗𝗮𝘁𝗮 𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗿𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻: Employ robust compression algorithms at the source for all outbound data.
*   𝗣𝗿𝗶𝘃𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗿𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗻𝗲𝗰𝘁𝘀: Leverage services like AWS Direct Connect or Azure ExpressRoute for predictable performance and often lower rates than public internet.
*   𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗹𝗹𝗶𝗴𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗖𝗮𝗰𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴: Strategically cache frequently accessed data in regional hubs to reduce redundant transfers.

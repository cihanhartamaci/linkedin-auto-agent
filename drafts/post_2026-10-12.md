Accurate Available-to-Promise (ATP) and Capable-to-Promise (CTP) figures are often the single biggest integration challenge in high-volume supply chains.

Deriving reliable ATP/CTP means harmonizing real-time inventory from WMS (physical, allocated, inbound), OMS (reserved, backordered), and ERP (financial, quality hold, future POs). This isn't just data sync; it's a dynamic calculation across multiple systems, often with conflicting states or latency.

This service aggregates inventory facts, applies business rules (e.g., safety stock, pre-allocations), and calculates commitment windows. Ensure a clear 𝘀𝗼𝘂𝗿𝗰𝗲-𝗼𝗳-𝘁𝗿𝘂𝘁𝗵 𝗵𝗶𝗲𝗿𝗮𝗿𝗰𝗵𝘆 for inventory segments. For CTP, integrate future supply (PO/production schedules) and use a lightweight planning engine within or alongside the IAS. Prioritize 𝗔𝗣𝗜-𝗳𝗶𝗿𝘀𝘁 𝗶𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗴𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 for high-frequency updates from WMS/OMS, and consider event-driven patterns for low-latency state changes. This minimizes costly, direct calls to ERP or WMS for every ATP/CTP query.

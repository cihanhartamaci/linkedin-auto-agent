A generic "return received" status is insufficient. Lacking detailed condition codes (A-stock, B-stock, defective) and final disposition (restock, repair, scrap) directly jeopardizes ERP financial reconciliation and inventory valuation accuracy. This impacts profitability.

Design for explicit, condition-driven disposition pathways.

*   𝗢𝗠𝗦: Track RMA status against original order, enabling customer communication.
*   𝗘𝗥𝗣 (𝗡𝗲𝘁𝗦𝘂𝗶𝘁𝗲/𝗦𝗔𝗣): Requires specific inventory adjustments (location, bin, status, value), accounting entries, and credit memo triggers based on WMS disposition. Implement 𝗵𝘆𝗯𝗿𝗶𝗱 𝗔𝗣𝗜/𝗘𝗗𝗜: APIs for real-time RMA initiation and status updates; EDI 846/867 for bulk inventory adjustments from 3PLs post-inspection. Focus on 𝗶𝗻𝘃𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗼𝗿𝘆 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝘁𝘂𝘀 𝗶𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗴𝗿𝗶𝘁𝘆.

Reconciling EDI 846 Inventory Advice in a multi-source logistics network is more than just processing a file; it's a data integrity battle. When multiple 3PLs or internal warehouses send 846s to a central OMS/ERP, inconsistencies are inevitable.

Simple "last-in wins" or blind updates lead to inaccurate stock positions and fulfillment errors. The challenge isn't receiving the 846, but architecting a reliable system to detect and resolve discrepancies against your system of record, or even across various internal/external sources simultaneously. This impacts customer experience and operational efficiency directly.

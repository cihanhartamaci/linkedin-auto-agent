Without robust idempotent API design, system retries (due to timeouts, network glitches, or temporary unavailability) lead to disastrous outcomes: double-shipping, incorrect stock counts, or financial reconciliation nightmares. This isn't just an "error handling" problem; it's a fundamental design decision for resilient distributed systems.

Navigating multi-channel returns is a critical architectural challenge, far beyond simple product reception. It demands orchestrating precise financial and inventory reconciliation across disparate WMS, OMS, and ERP systems.

The core issue: each platform possesses a fragmented view. WMS manages physical receipts and disposition. OMS tracks customer orders, return authorizations, and status updates. ERP handles financial reversals, credit memos, and general ledger impacts. Desynchronization here directly impacts profitability via inaccurate inventory, delayed refunds, or compliance risks.

Implement an event-driven pattern: `ReturnInitiated` triggers RMA creation; `ReturnReceived` updates WMS/OMS for physical processing; `ReturnDispositioned` informs ERP for credit memo; `RefundIssued` finalizes OMS/ERP.

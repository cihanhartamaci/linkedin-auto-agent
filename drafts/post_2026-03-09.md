Inconsistent product and customer master data silently cripples logistics operations. Imagine a WMS receiving an item with incorrect dimensions, or an OMS struggling with fragmented customer profiles. This isn't just a data quality issue; it's an architectural challenge demanding a dedicated 𝗠𝗗𝗠 𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗴𝘆.

Without a single source of truth, disparate WMS, OMS, and ERP systems (NetSuite, SAP) propagate errors. This leads to mis-picks, fulfillment delays, invoicing discrepancies, and a fractured customer experience. Integration efforts become fragile, constantly patching downstream data rather than sourcing it reliably.

Our approach involves establishing a centralized 𝗠𝗗𝗠 𝗵𝘂𝗯 with defined Systems of Record (SOR) for critical attributes. This hub then orchestrates consistent data synchronization via APIs to all consuming systems. For supplier product data, hybridizing EDI 832 (Catalog) ingestion into the MDM and subsequent API distribution ensures upstream accuracy. For customer data, CRM feeds the MDM, then distributes to OMS/ERP. This ensures data integrity at scale.

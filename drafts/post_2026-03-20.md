Orchestrating 𝗸𝗶𝘁𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗮𝘀𝘀𝗲𝗺𝗯𝗹𝘆 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸𝗳𝗹𝗼𝘄𝘀 for configurable products is more than just inventory management; it's a bidirectional choreography between OMS and WMS. Failure to design a robust integration here leads to costly build errors and fulfillment delays.

Standard order-to-ship processes often fall short when products require pre-shipment assembly or kitting based on customer-specific configurations. The OMS must accurately translate complex product options into a dynamic Bill of Materials (BOM) for the WMS. Conversely, the WMS needs to report component consumption and assembly status back to OMS/ERP for accurate costing and inventory reconciliation. This demands careful API design and event-driven communication.

Prioritize:
*   𝗗𝘆𝗻𝗮𝗺𝗶𝗰 𝗕𝗢𝗠 𝗧𝗿𝗮𝗻𝘀𝗺𝗶𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻: OMS sends assembly instructions and component lists to WMS, not just a SKU.
*   𝗪𝗠𝗦 𝗔𝗰𝗸𝗻𝗼𝘄𝗹𝗲𝗱𝗴𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 & 𝗦𝘁𝗮𝘁𝘂𝘀: WMS confirms receipt, provides real-time updates on component picks, assembly progress, and finished goods creation.
*   𝗕𝗮𝗰𝗸𝗳𝗹𝘂𝘀𝗵 & 𝗖𝗼𝗻𝘀𝘂𝗺𝗽𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗘𝘃𝗲𝗻𝘁𝘀: WMS triggers events for component consumption, impacting ERP inventory for accurate COGS.
*   𝗘𝗿𝗿𝗼𝗿 𝗛𝗮𝗻𝗱𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴: Clear mechanisms for WMS to signal component shortages or assembly issues back to OMS for order modification.

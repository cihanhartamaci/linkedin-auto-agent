Relying on strict real-time transactional consistency for global inventory visibility across dozens of fulfillment centers and sales channels is often a performance bottleneck, not a feature.

In high-scale multi-tenant, multi-DC logistics, immediate consistency becomes prohibitively expensive. We frequently deal with hundreds of thousands of inventory updates hourly, across various WMS, OMS, and ERP instances (NetSuite, SAP). Focus on strong eventual consistency guarantees for critical path data, allowing eventual reconciliation for non-critical attributes. Implement conflict resolution strategies using last-writer-wins or domain-specific merge rules.

How do you manage global inventory consistency across your highly distributed logistics network without compromising performance?

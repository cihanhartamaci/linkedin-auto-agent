High-volume inventory updates from a WMS can cripple an ERP. Pushing every granular stock change in real-time is often an anti-pattern.

ERPs like NetSuite or SAP aren't designed for thousands of concurrent, fine-grained inventory adjustments. This often leads to locking, deadlocks, and performance degradation.

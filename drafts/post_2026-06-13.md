The silent killer in 3PL integrations isn't always missing data, but *misaligned* data between the EDI 940 (Warehouse Shipping Order) and its corresponding 945 (Warehouse Shipping Advice).

When a 3PL's WMS partially fulfills a 940, ships an alternative SKU, or cancels an item post-allocation, the resulting 945 can wreak havoc on an ERP's inventory counts and financial postings. Without robust reconciliation, you face COGS discrepancies, incorrect customer invoicing, and delayed inventory visibility.

Architect real-time validation layers. On 945 receipt, compare quantities, SKUs, and statuses against the original 940 and current ERP committed inventory. Implement automated discrepancy flagging for variances > 5% or critical fields. Leverage a message queue for retry logic and manual review queues for unresolvable issues.

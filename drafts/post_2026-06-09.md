Traditional monolithic middleware struggles under unpredictable bursts of order, inventory, or shipment updates. An event-driven serverless approach ensures inherent 𝗲𝗹𝗮𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗶𝘁𝘆 and 𝗱𝗲𝗰𝗼𝘂𝗽𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴. Each microservice processes a specific event (e.g., `OrderCreated`, `InventoryAdjusted`), scales independently, and fails gracefully without impacting unrelated workflows. This isolation is crucial for maintaining real-time data flow without bottlenecks.

Design your integration flows around discrete events. Utilize managed services for message queues (e.g., SQS, Kafka), serverless functions (e.g., Lambda, Azure Functions), and API gateways. Focus on small, single-purpose functions. Implement robust retry mechanisms and dead-letter queues at the service level. This shifts operational burden and allows teams to focus on core integration logic.

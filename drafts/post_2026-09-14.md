"Dark inventory" isn't a myth; it's a persistent operational and integration challenge. This occurs when physical stock exists in the WMS, yet remains invisible or unallocatable within the OMS or ERP due to integration gaps, timing issues, or process failures.

The impact is significant: missed sales, customer dissatisfaction, and inaccurate financial reporting. Standard real-time inventory updates often miss these nuanced discrepancies. Relying solely on 846 EDI or API updates isn't enough; true visibility requires a dedicated 𝗿𝗲𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗰𝗶𝗹𝗶𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗹𝗮𝘆𝗲𝗿.

We implement 𝗮𝘀𝘆𝗻𝗰𝗵𝗿𝗼𝗻𝗼𝘂𝘀 𝗿𝗲𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗰𝗶𝗹𝗶𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝘀𝗲𝗿𝘃𝗶𝗰𝗲𝘀 that proactively scan for discrepancies between WMS and OMS/ERP stock levels. This involves:
*   Regular, scheduled audits comparing system-of-record inventory snapshots.
*   Event-driven triggers for specific discrepancy types (e.g., WMS adjusts inventory, but ERP fails to confirm).
*   A "reconciliation workbench" for manual review of complex cases.
This ensures "dark" stock is quickly brought to light, preventing oversells and improving fulfillment rates.
